## 2. What each app's structure teaches

### Sunsama (26)
**Core idea:** the day is the unit. Everything flows into "today" through rituals.
- **Inbox / triage:** there is no Inbox. The Backlog (`081924`) groups loose tasks by time horizon: next week or two, month, quarter, year, someday, never. A "Braindump" button dumps text fast. Triage happens inside daily planning, not in a list.
- **List / board:** Home is a board whose columns are days (`081711`, `084907`, `084913`). Dragging a card to another day reschedules it. Past days dim. Each column shows total planned time.
- **Timeline:** none. Time lives in the calendar.
- **Saved views:** none. Channels (#work, #personal) act as categories (`084605`).
- **Completed:** done tasks stay in place, dimmed, with a filled green check (`082026`). Shutdown splits the day into "Worked on" and "Didn't get to" (`081916`).
- **Daily planning:** a guided flow (`081720`, `084800`). Step 1: pick tasks. Step 2: "What can wait?" with Today / Tomorrow / Next week columns and "Keep only what's essential". Set a shutdown time. Drop tasks onto the calendar.
- **Weekly:** weekly objectives panel (`082036`), weekly planning cards (`084647`), weekly review with bars and donut (`084558`).
- **Calendar:** tasks become timeboxes with done checks (`085013`, `085021`). A filter shows planned vs actual time (`084824`).
- **Focus:** single task, ACTUAL vs PLANNED, subtasks with times (`082105`). A running timer shows in the nav (`084907`).
- **Analytics:** short, honest. Says "Not much data" when that is true (`084936`).
- **Visual language:** low density, white canvas, thin grey dividers, small sans type, colour only in channel dots and the green progress bar. Motion is quiet. Rituals feel calm because each step has one question as a big heading.
- **Lesson for us:** planning as a short ritual with one question per step. Time totals everywhere. Planned vs actual. Honest empty data.

### Asana (26)
**Core idea:** projects with many views of the same tasks.
- **Inbox:** Inbox is a notification feed (`083206`) with Archive for read notifications. This is team plumbing. It is not task triage.
- **List:** My tasks groups by "Recently assigned / Do today / Do next week / Do later" (`083221`). Project lists group by section (`082629`, `083421`). Sections collapse. "Add task" sits inline at each section foot.
- **Board:** columns are sections with counts and "+ Add section" (`082620`). Cards show check, title, priority, status and date range.
- **Timeline / Gantt:** lanes per section, bars, today line, dependencies (`082555`, `083731`, `083823`).
- **Saved views:** each project has view tabs (List, Board, Calendar, Timeline, Dashboard). A wizard picks them with a live preview (`083308`, `083322`). Workload has "Save view" (`083801`).
- **Completed:** completed tasks are hidden by a filter. "Archive" only exists for notifications.
- **Goals:** nested goals with status pill and % bar (`083918`), time-period picker (`084011`), goal detail with progress chart and Today line (`084030`), sub-goals linked to parent (`084142`).
- **Project health:** On track / At risk / Off track and a status history (`083845`).
- **Home:** date eyebrow, "Good morning" headline, small stats, widgets (`083146`).
- **Task detail:** a right side pane, not a modal (`082802`, `082834`, `083112`).
- **Visual language:** medium density, thin borders, small type, coloured chips for priority and status, red for late dates, many avatars. Dark mode is clean. Motion is near zero.
- **Lesson for us:** side-pane detail, collapsible groups with inline add, goal status plus progress chart, time-period picker. Skip the team layer.

### monday.com (4)
**Core idea:** a coloured table where any column can group the board.
- **List:** groups by status with a coloured left edge and a stacked colour summary bar under each group (`082235`, `082250`).
- **Board:** Kanban can group by any field, here priority (`082421`, `082541`). Dragging changes that field.
- **Visual language:** very dense, fully saturated status cells. Loud. Reads as a spreadsheet.
- **Lesson for us:** "group by any field" is useful. The summary bar under a group is a nice touch at small size. Do not copy the full-cell saturated colour; it fights our one-accent rule.

### Morgen (likely, 4)
**Core idea:** tasks sidebar next to a calendar; plan by dragging tasks into time.
- **Inbox:** Inbox is the default list for new tasks with no list (`082122`, `091119`). Smart lists sit above it: Overdue, Due today, Due tomorrow, Due soon, each with a live count.
- **Task fields:** importance, estimate, earliest start, due date, repeat (`091119`). Earliest start vs due date matches our "scheduled vs due" split.
- **Command palette:** create anything, key caps shown, natural-language tokens (`082208`).
- **Lesson for us:** smart lists with counts are the cheapest form of saved views. "Earliest start" is a good label for our scheduled date.

### Fernly (BEST Shots, 5)
Concept SaaS. Team product, but its craft is the bar. Detail in section 5.
- Tasks has filter pills (All, My tasks, High priority, Due this week) instead of a dropdown (`B2`). The subtitle teaches the gesture: "Drag a card to another stage, or use its menu to move it."
- Calendar pairs a month grid with a right rail: today's agenda and "Coming up" (`B3`, `B5`).
- Analytics: period pill, deltas, comparison line, donut with total (`B4`).

### Coterie (BEST Shots, 2)
- One screen holds the whole day: greeting, progress pills, timer dial, week calendar, checklist (`B6`, `B7`).
- The single dark card (checklist) is the one heavy element. Everything else is light.

### Fitness "Tega" (BEST Shots, 1)
- Three layouts of the same home: rings, dark hero, bento (`B0`). Shows how one dataset can take three compositions.
- Routine rows: thumbnail, title, time and duration, check circle, "Up next" pill on the next item. A vertical connector line in the middle variant shows sequence.
- Bottom nav is a floating pill. The active tab expands into a labelled pill.

## 3. Pattern library by our page

Format: **pattern** (sources). What to copy. Why it fits a solo premium planner.

### Dashboard (done, the bar)
Already meets the bar. Use as the reference for the rest. Keep in mind:
1. **One hero card per screen** (`B1`, `B6`, `C-overview`). One filled card, the rest white. Our Dashboard already does this; other pages should copy the ratio.
2. **Widget header with a single "Open X →" link** (`C-overview`). Each widget leads into its page. Keep this as the cross-page entry pattern.
3. **Recorded-activity heatmap** (`C-overview`, `B4`). Shared component with Review and Habits.

### Today
1. **Day progress bar under the date** (`082026`, `084824`). A thin bar: done minutes vs planned minutes, labelled. Fits: one glance answers "how is today going".
2. **Running focus card at the top** (`084907`, `C-today`, `C-blue`). When a session runs, it becomes the hero; when not, the hero is "Plan day". Fits: one primary action at a time.
3. **Ordered routine rows with "Up next"** (`B0`). Next item gets a small pill; done items get a filled check and dim. Fits: a solo day is a sequence.
4. **Agenda rail "Your day"** (`C-today`, `C-blue`, `B3`). Times in IBM Plex Mono, current row tinted, category dot plus label.
5. **Totals in the header** (`081711`, `C-today`). "3 tasks · 1h 10m". Real data only.
6. **Recovery inline** (`081916`, `C-habits`). Late or missed items show "Move to tomorrow" and "Start again" right in the row, not hidden in a menu.

### Tasks (Inbox, List, Board, Saved views, Archived, bulk select)
1. **Tabs Inbox / List / Board, Archive pushed right** (`C-tasks`). Archive stays quiet and secondary.
2. **Triage chips on Inbox rows** (`081924`, `084558`). Today, This week, Next month, Someday, or a date. One click clears a row. Fits: capture fast, sort later.
3. **Collapsible groups with count and time total, inline add** (`083221`, `082629`, `C-tasks`). Group header: name, count, total estimate, chevron.
4. **Filter pills, not a filter dropdown** (`B2`, `082122`). Saved views render as pills with live counts. "All" first.
5. **Board columns with dot, name and count; menu move as drag alternative** (`B2`, `082620`). Cards: title, category chip, estimate, due. Done cards strike through and dim.
6. **Right side detail pane** (`082802`, `C-tasks`). Planned date, Deadline and Estimate as separate rows. "Start focus" is the primary button.
7. **Bulk select with undo** (no clear competitor shot; skill rule). Checkbox appears on hover or long-press; a floating action bar shows "Move to, Set date, Complete, Archive"; every bulk action shows an Undo toast.
- **Avoid (team-only):** assignee and collaborator columns, task visibility, comments, @mentions, "Add field" builder (`084353`), automations (`082855`), forms.

### Calendar (day / week / month)
1. **Unscheduled task panel beside the grid** (`082122`, `085013`, `085021`). Drag tasks into time slots to timeblock. Fits our timeblock model.
2. **Planned vs actual overlay toggle** (`084824`). Show the timeblock and the real focus time side by side. Ties Focus data to the calendar.
3. **Month grid with soft tile cells and a right rail** (`B3`, `B5`). Rail: "TODAY · WEEKDAY", big date, today's items, then "Coming up" with date tiles.
4. **Done checks on blocks** (`085013`). A finished block shows a filled check and dims.
5. **Now-line is the only pulse** (`081720`, `091119`). Already our rule.
6. **Hover "+ Add" on empty cells and slots** (`082609`). Opens a small popover like `091119` with estimate and earliest start.

### Plan (daily / weekly)
1. **One question per step** (`081720`, `084800`, `081557`). "What do you want to get done today?", then "What can wait?". Big heading, one task. Fits a calm solo ritual.
2. **Today / Tomorrow / Next week columns for cutting the day** (`084800`). Drag to defer. Column totals warn when today is over capacity.
3. **Shutdown time and capacity line** (`081720`, `081916`). Planned minutes vs available minutes as a bar with tick marks.
4. **Weekly objective banner** (`C-week`, `082036`, `084647`). One to three objectives for the week, linked to a goal or milestone.
5. **Week board of day columns with left rail of inbox / this week / later** (`C-week`, `081711`). Drop on a new day shows a small confirm ("Move to Thursday? Review / Cancel") only when it would break a timeblock; otherwise an Undo toast.
6. **Daily shutdown review** (`081916`, `084703`). "Worked on / Didn't get to", each leftover gets a one-click destination.

### Roadmap
1. **Project lanes with phase bars and today line** (`C-timeline`, `082555`, `083731`).
2. **Milestones as diamonds, dependencies as thin elbow arrows** (`C-timeline`, `083112`). Keep dependency display, skip "Blocked by" columns.

## 4. Tasks answers

### What is the Inbox for?
- **Asana:** Inbox = notifications (`083206`). Not tasks. Team only.
- **Morgen:** Inbox = default list for tasks with no list (`082122`, `091119`).
- **Sunsama:** no Inbox. Backlog grouped by horizon (W, M, Q, Y, S, N) plus Braindump (`081924`). Triage happens in daily planning (`084800`).
- **Our concepts:** Inbox is the first Tasks tab (`C-tasks`) and the top of the Plan rail (`C-week`).
- **Fit for us:** Inbox = capture tray. A task sits there until it has a home: a date, a horizon, or a project. Every row has triage chips (Today, This week, Next month, Someday, Pick date, Project). Target is empty. ⌘K capture lands here. Notifications never go here.

### Dragging on the Board
- **Asana:** columns are sections; drag changes section (`082620`).
- **monday:** board groups by any field; drag changes that field (`082421`).
- **Sunsama:** columns are days; drag reschedules (`081711`).
- **Fernly:** drag or "use its menu to move it" (`B2`).
- **Our concept:** drop on a day asks "Move to Thursday? Review / Cancel" (`C-week`).
- **Fit for us:** Board with a "Group by" switch: Status (To do / Doing / Done) or Day (this week plus Unscheduled). Drag changes only the field the column stands for. Every card also has "Move to…" in its menu for keyboard and touch. Confirm only when a move would break a timeblock or pass the deadline; otherwise move and show Undo. Reuse `useTaskDnd`.

### List grouping
- **Asana:** by time bucket (Do today / next week / later, `083221`) or by section (`082629`).
- **monday:** by status, with a summary bar (`082235`).
- **Morgen:** smart lists by due bucket (`082122`).
- **Fit for us:** default group by When: Overdue, Today, This week, Later, Someday, No date. Group headers show count and total estimate, and collapse. Other groupings: Project, Category, Status. Overdue uses the warning colour plus a "Reschedule" action, not red alarm.

### Saved views
- **Asana:** per-project view tabs and "Save view" (`083221`, `083801`).
- **Morgen:** smart lists with counts (`082122`).
- **Fernly:** filter pills (`B2`).
- **Our concept:** sidebar Views (Today, This Week, Next Month, Someday) and a Saved tab (`C-tasks`).
- **Fit for us:** a saved view = filter + group + sort + layout (List or Board) with a name. Shown as pills under the tabs, with live counts. Ship a few built-in smart views (for example Overdue, Due soon, No estimate) and let the owner pin their own. Drop the separate "Saved" tab.

### Archive and completed
- **Sunsama:** done tasks stay in place, dimmed with a filled check (`082026`). Shutdown lists what got done (`081916`).
- **Asana:** completed hidden by filter. "Archive" is for notifications.
- **Fernly:** Done column with strikethrough (`B2`).
- **Our concepts:** Archive tab right-aligned (`C-tasks`); "Completed" in the views list (`C-timeline`).
- **Fit for us:** split two ideas. **Completed** = done. Done items dim in place for the rest of the day, then leave active lists. **Archived** = not done, but put away on purpose (paused project, dropped idea). Both live behind the quiet right-aligned Archive tab, with a Completed / Archived switch, search, and Restore.

### Which model fits us best
A blend. **Morgen-style capture** (Inbox as a tray), **Sunsama-style horizons and day columns** (triage by time, drag to reschedule), and **Asana-style view switching** (List and Board on the same data, side detail pane). Leave out everything team-shaped.

## 5. What makes the BEST Shots and concepts premium

### Shared traits (measured by eye from the shots)
- **Radii.** Cards about 16 to 20px. The page tray about 24px. Chips and tags about 6 to 8px. All buttons and filters are full pills. Small icon buttons are 28 to 32px circles (`B1` arrow buttons). Maps to our chip 8, control 12, panel 16, feature 24, pill 999.
- **Nested tray (double bezel).** Fernly's content sits in a light grey tray on a dark backdrop, and white cards sit in that tray (`B1` to `B5`). Coterie does the same on cream (`B6`). Separation comes from tone, not borders.
- **Shadows.** Almost none. Very soft and wide when present. No hard edges. Matches our ink-tinted `--pc-e1`.
- **Type scale.** Page title 28 to 32px, tight tracking. KPI numbers 36 to 48px, tight. Card titles 14 to 16px semibold. Labels 12 to 13px muted. Numbers use tabular figures. Three or four sizes per screen only.
- **Colour.** One hue family per app (Fernly green, Coterie amber, Tega coral). One filled hero card, everything else white. Category colour only in small chips and dots. Hatched fill for "not yet" or "empty" (`B1` bars, `B6` progress pill).
- **Icons.** Thin line icons, one stroke width, small (16 to 18px). Arrow-in-circle for "open" (`B1`, `B6`).
- **Card composition.** Title top left, one action top right, a big number or a short list, one footer line. Lots of air. Never more than one chart per card.
- **Data viz.** Rounded bar caps, a value tag on the active bar (`B1` "74%"), comparison as a dashed line (`B4`), donut with total in the centre (`B4`), heatmap with Less / More key (`B4`, `C-habits`), dial with tick marks (`B6`, `C-focus`).
- **Status.** Done = strikethrough plus filled check plus dimmed (`B2`, `B7`). "Up next" = small coloured pill (`B0`).
- **Motion.** The `B5` title says "Dashboard Animations!". Motion is entry stagger, count-up numbers and chart growth. Calm, short.

## 6. Visual direction for the restructure

1. **One hero per screen.** At most one filled blue or night card. Everything else is a white panel on the page tone.
2. **Tone, not lines.** Separate areas by surface tone and soft ink-tinted shadow. Use hairlines only inside lists.
3. **Keep the radius ladder.** Chip 8, control 12, panel 16, feature 24, pill 999. Nested corners stay concentric.
4. **Three type sizes per card.** Title, value, label. Big numbers use tabular figures and tight tracking. Timing stays in IBM Plex Mono.
5. **Pills for choices.** Filters, saved views, periods and segment switches are pills with live counts. No filter dropdown as the first step.
6. **Colour always has words.** Category, status and priority show a dot or chip plus a label. Late uses the warning tone plus an action.
7. **Done looks done.** Filled green check, dim, strikethrough. It stays in place until the day ends.
8. **Empty and missing are honest.** Hatched or ghost fills for "not yet". Real empty states with one next step. No fake numbers.
9. **Thin icons, one set.** One line weight, 16 to 20px, arrow-in-circle for "open". No emoji.
10. **Every page has a side pane or sheet for detail.** Side pane on desktop, sheet on phone. Never a full-page jump for a task.
11. **Motion explains change.** Entry stagger 40ms (cap 8), count-up and chart growth on the data ease, drag lift and drop settle on the UI ease. Pulse only on the now-line. Reduced motion turns it all off.
12. **No team furniture.** No avatars, assignees, comments, invites or workload. The space goes to time totals and recovery actions.
